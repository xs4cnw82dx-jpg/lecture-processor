From two sources to one document

Less switching between
slides and recordings.

A slide often contains the outline; the spoken explanation supplies the context. Lecture Notes brings those sources together so you can review one connected document.

  1. 1

    Add your sources

    Upload PDF or PPTX slides and an audio recording. You can also record in your browser or import a supported lecture link.

  2. 2

    Transcribe, extract, combine

    The recording becomes a transcript. Text is extracted from the slides. Both are combined into structured notes.

  3. 3

    Review and make it yours

    Read and edit the result, then use optional flashcards and practice questions to work with the material.
