Privacy in xPEDitie Pijn Studio

Taking an expedition

You can open a shared expedition without an account. Your browser saves your progress on this device so you can return to the course.

Sharing in a pilot

If an expedition collects responses, you can participate privately or choose to share your progress, answers and feedback with the course authors. Shared responses are associated with a session ID. The player shows when other learners can see group results or submitted words.

You can switch to private participation during the expedition. The authors keep the responses you shared before switching. Clearing your browser’s saved course progress removes your local copy.

Fonts in the Studio

Studio pages for authors load two weights of the General Sans typeface from Fontshare, a service of the Indian Type Foundry. Opening these pages sends your IP address and browser details to Fontshare's servers. Learner pages do not load these fonts.

Author accounts and courses

The Studio uses Firebase Authentication for sign-in. Course records and pilot responses are stored in Firestore. Uploaded course media is stored in Supabase. Authors can grant collaborators access to an expedition and choose when to share a learner link.

Questions about your data

For questions about a pilot or a shared response, contact the course organizer who sent your expedition link.
