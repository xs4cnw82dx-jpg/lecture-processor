Keep your lecture close

Copy the recording’s playlist URL, choose a format, and save a copy for later.

  1. Open your lecture recording and press play.
  2. Open DevTools: Cmd + Option + I on Mac, Ctrl + Shift + I on Windows, or right-click the page and choose Inspect.
  3. Open Network, filter on index.m3u8, and copy the playlist request URL. If no request appears, reload the recording with Network open and press play again.
  4. Paste the copied URL here, choose video, audio or both, then download.
